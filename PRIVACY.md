# Sugar Lock – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

Sugar Lock is a free, open-source app made by Lewis Mcilreavy. It shows the glucose reading from the CamAPS FX app on your lock screen. Sugar Lock is not made by or affiliated with CamDiab Ltd.

## What the app reads

Sugar Lock uses Android's notification access to read **only the notifications posted by the CamAPS FX app**. Notifications from other apps are ignored as soon as they arrive. They are not read, stored or shared.

From the CamAPS FX notification it keeps:
- your latest glucose value, unit and the time it arrived
- a short history of recent values (about the last 25 minutes), used to work out the trend arrow
- running totals of time spent low, in range and high today, used for "time in range"
- the text of the last CamAPS notification, shown in the app to help fix reading problems

It also stores your target range and which daily joke has been shown.

## Where your data goes

**Nowhere.** All of the above is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage.

Sugar Lock:
- has **no internet permission** and cannot send data anywhere
- has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking
- does not share data with anyone, including the developer

## Deleting your data

Clear the app's storage (**Settings > Apps > Sugar Lock > Storage > Clear data**) or uninstall the app. This deletes everything it has stored.

## Children

Sugar Lock does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

## Medical disclaimer

Sugar Lock is a display only and is not a medical device. It does not replace CamAPS FX, its alarms, or advice from your diabetes team. Always check CamAPS FX before making treatment decisions.
