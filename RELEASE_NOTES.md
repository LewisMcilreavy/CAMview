## What's new

- **No more uninstalling.** The APK is now signed with a fixed key, so future versions install straight over the top. You'll need to uninstall one last time when moving to this build.
- **New name.** The project now lives at github.com/LewisMcilreavy/Sugar-Lock. Old links still redirect.
- **New logo.** A padlock with a blood-drop keyhole, as the app icon (including Android's themed icons).
- **Getting ready for Google Play.** Builds count up their version number, and there's a privacy policy.

## Install

1. Tap **SugarLock.apk** below to download it.
2. Open it and tap **Install**. If Android blocks it, allow installs from your browser. If Play Protect warns you, choose **Install anyway**.
3. Updating keeps your settings and widget.

New to Sugar Lock? Follow the [setup guide](https://github.com/LewisMcilreavy/Sugar-Lock#2-first-time-setup).

> Sugar Lock is a display only. CamAPS FX stays in charge of alarms and insulin. Always check CamAPS before acting on a number.
