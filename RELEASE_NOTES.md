## What's new

- **Trend arrow fix.** Dexcom readings arrive every 5 minutes, sometimes a few seconds early, which stopped the arrow from appearing. It now shows about 10 minutes after the app starts.
- **Full user guide.** Setup, the lock-screen widget, the Now Bar, target range and troubleshooting are now all in the [README](https://github.com/LewisMcilreavy/CAMview#readme).

## Install

1. Tap **SugarLock.apk** below to download it.
2. Open it and tap **Install**. If Android blocks it, allow installs from your browser. If Play Protect warns you, choose **Install anyway**.
3. Updating keeps your settings and widget.

New to Sugar Lock? Follow the [setup guide](https://github.com/LewisMcilreavy/CAMview#2-first-time-setup).

> Sugar Lock is a display only. CamAPS FX stays in charge of alarms and insulin. Always check CamAPS before acting on a number.
